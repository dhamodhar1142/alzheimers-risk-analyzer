# 🧠 Alzheimer's Risk Analyzer

An interactive web application that helps assess potential risk factors for Alzheimer's disease based on age, gender, lifestyle factors, and other health indicators.

## ✨ Features

- **Comprehensive Risk Assessment**: Analyzes multiple factors including:
  - Age and Gender
  - Family History
  - Lifestyle Factors (Exercise, Smoking, Alcohol)
  - Health Conditions (Diabetes, Hypertension, Heart Disease)
  - Cognitive & Lifestyle Indicators (Education, Social Activity, Sleep Quality)

- **Personalized Recommendations**: Get tailored suggestions for maintaining brain health
- **Risk Score Calculation**: Receive a detailed risk assessment based on your inputs
- **User-Friendly Interface**: Easy-to-use sidebar for inputting information
- **Educational Tool**: Learn about various risk factors and prevention strategies

## 📋 How to Use

1. Visit the app using the button above
2. Fill in your personal information in the sidebar:
   - Age
   - Gender
   - Family history of Alzheimer's
3. Provide lifestyle information:
   - Exercise frequency
   - Smoking status
   - Alcohol consumption
4. Indicate any health conditions
5. Share cognitive and lifestyle details
6. Click "Analyze Risk" to see your results
7. Review your risk assessment and personalized recommendations

## 🛡️ Disclaimer

This tool is for **educational and informational purposes only**. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. The risk assessment is based on general risk factors and should not be considered a definitive medical evaluation.

**Always consult with qualified healthcare professionals for medical advice, diagnosis, and treatment.**
